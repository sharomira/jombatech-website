Course Modules

Module 01

Fundamentals & Electronics Basics

  • Introduction to electrical quantities: Voltage, Current, and Resistance
  • How to use a Digital Multimeter (DMM) correctly
  • Identifying components: Capacitors, Resistors, Diodes, and Transistors
  • Safety precautions and Static Discharge (ESD) handling
Module 02

Hardware Disassembly & Screen / Battery Replacement

  • Safe teardown techniques for modern glued smartphones
  • Laptop disassembly, thermal pasting, and fan cleaning
  • Display unit replacement (AMOLED, LCD, Touch Digitizers)
  • Battery charging circuit analysis and battery swaps
Module 03

Soldering & Micro-Soldering Techniques

  • Soldering iron vs. Hot Air Rework Station techniques
  • Replacing charging ports (Micro-USB, Type-C, DC Jacks)
  • Jumper wire connections for broken traces
  • SMD component removal and replacement
Module 04

Advanced Troubleshooting & Software Flashing

  • Finding short circuits using a DC Power Supply
  • Smartphone software flashing, unbricking, and FRP unlocks
  • Laptop BIOS flashing and power sequence diagnosis
  • Customer handling and repair business management
